Factorii Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Developer: nothing-complex
Android application ID: com.factorii

Factorii does not collect, transmit, sell or share any user information. Your game stays on your device.

Information collected by the game

Factorii is an offline, single-player game. It does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, gameplay analytics or other user data. The game does not send information to the developer or to third parties.

No account or sign-in is required. Factorii contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or automatic crash-reporting services. The current Android game does not request Internet access.

Local storage, retention and deletion

High scores, game progress and settings are saved locally in the app's private storage so they can be available when you play again. These local files are not collected by the developer, uploaded or shared by the game. Android app backup is disabled for this version.

Local data remains on your device until removed. To delete it, clear Factorii's app storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. This permanently removes the local scores, progress and settings. We do not operate a server containing your game data, and there is no online account to delete.

Children's privacy

The same no-collection practices apply to all players, including children. Factorii does not ask players to provide personal information.

Optional community and support contact

The store listing may invite you to share a screenshot of your high score in the Factorii Reddit community. Sharing is entirely optional and happens outside the game. Factorii does not connect to your Reddit account or post anything for you. Reddit's own privacy policy applies when you use Reddit.

If you choose to email the developer, your email address and the information you include are used to respond to your inquiry. This information is provided voluntarily outside the game and is not collected automatically by Factorii. Please do not include sensitive information. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the address below, subject to any legal retention requirements.

This policy page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages and contains no added analytics, tracking scripts or advertising. Visiting this website is separate from playing Factorii. GitHub's hosting service is covered by its privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If the game's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will be revised.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact nothing-complex at nothing.complex@outlook.com.
